# WEOS Terms

This glossary scopes terms instead of forcing one definition where repository
sources conflict.

| Term                 | Canonical definition if available                                                      | Runtime usage                                                                                                                    | Conflicting uses                                                                        | Not equivalent to                             | Evidence                                                                           | Unresolved ambiguity                                                      |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `APPROVED`           | A governed decision on an exact artifact/revision in `WEOS-IMP-003`                    | `CaseEditorialStatus.APPROVED`; `DiagnosisEducationStatus.APPROVED`; graph candidate `APPROVED`; teaching rule string `APPROVED` | Sometimes status projection, sometimes decision result, sometimes pre-publication state | `PUBLISHED`, `ACTIVE`, `READY_TO_PUBLISH`     | `schema.prisma`; `WEOS-IMP-004-legacy-status-crosswalk.md`                         | Which runtime approvals have canonical decision records                   |
| `ACTIVE`             | Standing for active artifacts or operational visibility depending artifact             | `DiagnosisRegistryStatus.ACTIVE`; `DiagnosisGraphFactStatus.ACTIVE`; teaching/evidence relationship active                       | Can mean dictionary-visible, fact usable, teaching relationship usable, or rule active  | `APPROVED`, `PUBLISHED`                       | `schema.prisma`; lifecycle docs                                                    | Whether every active transition requires same authority                   |
| `READY_TO_PUBLISH`   | Readiness, not publication                                                             | `CaseEditorialStatus.READY_TO_PUBLISH` after `markReadyToPublish`                                                                | Legacy/current status may be mistaken for publication authorization                     | `PUBLISHED`, publication decision             | `case-review.service.ts`; `WEOS-IMP-004`                                           | Whether readiness decision is persisted canonically                       |
| `PUBLISHED`          | Publication standing must target exact published version                               | `CaseEditorialStatus.PUBLISHED`; `DiagnosisEducationStatus.PUBLISHED`; `DailyCase` exposes published cases                       | Case/education status and daily schedule can be conflated                               | Approval, schedule, release, learner exposure | `schema.prisma`; `WEOS-IMP-002`; `WEOS-IMP-005`                                    | Published artifact version/history model open                             |
| `REVIEW_REQUIRED`    | Phase/doc disposition or validation/trust state needing review                         | Phase 2 docs disposition; AI/reasoning statuses                                                                                  | Document review and content review share phrase                                         | `NEEDS_REVIEW`, rejection, failure            | `WEOS-IMP-005`; `schema.prisma`                                                    | Whether doc status and runtime status vocabularies should remain distinct |
| `NEEDS_REVIEW`       | Artifact lifecycle state requiring review                                              | Education status, candidate status, runtime string statuses                                                                      | Similar to `REVIEW_REQUIRED` but not identical                                          | `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `NEEDS_EDIT`               | `schema.prisma`; `WEOS-IMP-004`                                                    | Controlled vocabulary for string fields                                   |
| `apply`              | Controlled Application of accepted candidate, not approval                             | Clue draft apply mutates case and creates `CaseRevision`                                                                         | May be read as approval or publication by humans                                        | publish, approve, accept                      | `diagnosis-editorial-workspace.service.ts`; `WEOS-IMP-003`                         | Generic controlled application record absent                              |
| `publish`            | Publication/release/exposure are separate canonical concepts                           | Case daily publishing, education publish status, ready-to-publish                                                                | Often mixed with schedule/release/readiness                                             | approve, ready, schedule, expose              | `daily-cases.service.ts`; `diagnosis-education.service.ts`; `WEOS-IMP-002`         | Generic publication history/version model open                            |
| `candidate`          | Proposed artifact pending review                                                       | Registry candidate, graph candidate, teaching rule candidate                                                                     | Candidate may later create fact/registry/rule but is not itself final                   | fact, active registry, approved record        | `schema.prisma`; graph/registry services                                           | Promotion and approval boundaries                                         |
| `fact`               | Promoted graph knowledge artifact                                                      | `DiagnosisGraphFact` active/archived                                                                                             | Fact can be created during candidate approval                                           | candidate, evidence node                      | `schema.prisma`; `diagnosis-graph-candidates.service.ts`                           | Whether fact creation needs separate authority                            |
| `readiness`          | Evaluation of prerequisites, not lifecycle itself                                      | Diagnosis lifecycle readiness, publication readiness, reasoning readiness score                                                  | May be shown as status in dashboards                                                    | approval, active, published                   | `diagnosis-registry-lifecycle-policy.service.ts`; `workflowNavigationViewModel.ts` | Which readiness checks must persist records                               |
| `standing`           | Current lifecycle/visibility/permission state of existing artifact                     | Encoded in status booleans and canonical metadata `changesStandingOfArtifactTypes`                                               | Often implemented as mutable fields                                                     | created record, immutable decision            | `canonical-actions.ts`; `WEOS-IMP-003`                                             | Which standing changes need audit records                                 |
| `governance record`  | Record of why, authority, rationale, effect                                            | Case review decisions/context snapshots; many other domains lack generic record                                                  | Sometimes service logs or row fields stand in                                           | audit event alone, status field alone         | `case-review-governance.repository.ts`; `WEOS-IMP-005`                             | Generic vs typed record design                                            |
| `editorial decision` | Governed outcome under authority                                                       | `CaseEditorialDecision`; review decisions; canonical `EDITORIAL_DECISION`                                                        | Runtime `ReviewDecision` and status transitions are not always separate records         | review workflow, assessment, validation       | `schema.prisma`; `WEOS-IMP-003`                                                    | Cross-artifact decision envelope                                          |
| `audit event`        | Event that something happened                                                          | `CaseReviewEvent`, service logger events                                                                                         | Logs may be confused with persisted audit records                                       | governance record, editorial decision         | `case-review-governance.repository.ts`; services                                   | Which logs are sufficient, if any                                         |
| `revision`           | Versioned content snapshot                                                             | `CaseRevision`, `DiagnosisEducationRevision`; version fields on other models                                                     | Some artifacts have versions without revision tables                                    | current mutable row, approval                 | `schema.prisma`; `WEOS-IMP-005`                                                    | Teaching/reasoning revision models open                                   |
| `projection`         | Mutable compatibility/current state derived from or standing in for canonical concepts | `Case.editorialStatus`, `approvedAt`, `DiagnosisRegistry` flags                                                                  | Projections may be mistaken for canonical records                                       | governance record, immutable history          | `WEOS-IMP-001`; `WEOS-IMP-004`; `DATABASE-MODEL-MAP.md`                            | Synchronization strategy open                                             |
| `playable`           | Operational permission for diagnosis/case eligibility                                  | `DiagnosisRegistry.isPlayable`; case eligibility policy                                                                          | Can be conflated with active or published                                               | dictionary-visible, generatable, approved     | `schema.prisma`; `diagnosis-registry-lifecycle-policy.service.ts`                  | Expiry/review metadata open                                               |
| `dictionary-visible` | Runtime visibility in diagnosis dictionary/search                                      | `DiagnosisRegistry.status ACTIVE`, `active`, `isPlayable` in lifecycle telemetry                                                 | May be conflated with active registry alone                                             | playable, generatable, published              | `diagnosis-registry-lifecycle-policy.service.ts`                                   | Exact canonical artifact boundary                                         |
