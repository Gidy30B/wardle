Company position

DxLabs should read publicly as a focused health technology company, not a broad research lab. Wardle is the first product, the proof of direction, and the clearest public anchor.

Why DxLabs

From clinical knowledge to better practice.

Healthcare depends on how well knowledge becomes judgement, routine, coordination and action. DxLabs works in that gap with products that are clinically grounded, usable and measured.

Flagship product

Wardle is clinical reasoning, practised daily.

Wardle is a case-based learning platform for medical students and early-career clinicians. Learners work through progressive clinical information, commit to a diagnosis and review the reasoning behind the case.

Company direction

One company, four connected areas.

The public story should be directional but restrained: clear enough to understand the company, careful enough not to expose private product or infrastructure plans too early.

Current

Medical education

Learning products that strengthen clinical reasoning and purposeful practice, led by Wardle.

Selective

Health systems

Digital tools for coordinated clinical work, reliable information and accessible services where the use case is focused.

Long term

Diagnostic support

Responsible exploration of technology that improves how clinical information is interpreted and taught.

Research

Imaging science

Interest in the quality, usefulness and accessibility of medical imaging, without overstating public commitments.

Hosting plan direction

Publicly simple. Operationally ready.

The site should signal that DxLabs can host and operate real products, while keeping deployment details, security design and partner-specific environments out of the public copy.

Company web

Use this page as the public DxLabs home, with Wardle as the primary product pathway and a restrained company narrative.

Product domain

Keep Wardle on its own product domain so learners and institutions have a direct product destination.

Platform posture

Describe production readiness at a high level: web app delivery, account access, analytics and support workflows.

Institutional path

Frame institutional deployments as considered case by case, without publishing non-public technical or commercial terms.

Public alignment

Say enough to build trust. Hold enough to stay strategic.

Say clearly

  • DxLabs is clinician-led and grounded in healthcare experience.
  • Wardle is the flagship product and the current public focus.
  • The company is interested in medical education, health systems and responsible clinical technology.
  • The work is shaped by African healthcare context and practical constraints.

Keep directional

  • Specific hosting architecture, vendors and deployment details.
  • Unannounced partnerships, institutional pilots or commercial terms.
  • Experimental diagnostic, imaging or AI work before it is ready.
  • Internal product roadmap beyond broad areas of interest.
